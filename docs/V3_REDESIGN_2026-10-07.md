# Opportunity Engine V3 redesign — 2026-10-07

## Goal

Rebuild V3 as the future unified opportunity engine while preserving the current V2 production baseline for rollback.

The redesign follows four rules:

1. **Broad discovery, strict execution.** Discovery is optimized for recall; purchase/mail policy remains conservative.
2. **One ExecutionProof model.** Every deep candidate records source cost, destination value/depth, taxes, hauling, access, stress result, coverage, timestamp, net profit, ROI and profit density before policy classification.
3. **Mutually exclusive exit routes.** Pure item contracts are routed in confidence order: `FULL_CASH` → `PARTIAL_CASH_FLOOR` → `LIST-SUPPORTED`. Barter remains a separate executable path.
4. **Observable rejection funnel.** Every run records stage counts and rejection reasons so low alert volume can be diagnosed without reading logs manually.

## Preserved production safety rules

- Formal mail profit floor: **50M ISK**.
- Formal non-BPC profit density: **2,000 ISK/m³**.
- SAFE still requires positive stress profit and verified access.
- Unknown/unfriendly player structures remain fail-closed.
- Capital/high-sec restricted assets and SKIN-heavy bundles remain protected.
- Conservative listing is **WATCH-only**; it never becomes a cash SAFE or automatic-buy recommendation.
- BPC and 4-H policy are not relaxed by this redesign.

## Candidate discovery

Default deep-validation pool expands from 300 to 600 candidates. Ranking is diversified across:

- snapshot profit,
- ROI,
- conservative-listing economics,
- new/changed contract fingerprint,
- newly issued contracts,
- rotating exploration,
- product diversity.

Contract fingerprints include contract id, price, included/requested items, location and expiry. New or changed contracts receive priority; unchanged contracts remain eligible through economic and exploration buckets.

## Policy states

`PolicyEngine` returns:

- `RESEARCH` — interesting but not yet executable/reliable enough,
- `WATCH` — evidence exists but risk/uncertainty prevents SAFE,
- `SAFE` — execution proof passes strict safety rules but is below the formal notification gate,
- `MAIL` — SAFE plus the formal user-facing gate.

Output rows retain `execution_status` for compatibility and add `policy_stage`, `confidence_class`, `mail_eligible`, and `policy_reason`.

## Safe rollout

V2 is preserved in branch `archive/v2-production-2026-10-07`.

During parallel validation, `V3_FULL_CASH_MAIL_ENABLED=0` prevents the new V3 FULL_CASH channel from duplicating V2 production alerts. The redesign can therefore be shadow-tested without changing the current purchase recommendation stream.

Cutover should happen only after:

1. V3 CI is green.
2. Shadow runs show no material regression in V2-equivalent FULL_CASH proofs.
3. Rejection funnel counts are stable and explainable.
4. Candidate recall increases without increasing false SAFE/MAIL decisions.
5. Only then enable V3 FULL_CASH mail and retire the overlapping V2/Multi public-contract path.

## Not in this change
